Delete your Sunny Harvest data

Sunny Harvest (com.fatalbug.idlefarmtycoon) · Developer: FatalBug

Sunny Harvest has no accounts of its own, so there is no account to close. You can erase your game data yourself in the game, or ask us by email.

Option 1: in the game (immediate)

  1. Open Sunny Harvest and tap Settings.
  2. Press and hold Reset Profile until it confirms.
  3. Your farm progress is erased on this device, and your Google Play Games cloud save is replaced with a fresh farm. Other devices signed in to the same Google account pick up the reset too.

Option 2: by email

  1. Email shukurov.nurlan.1991@gmail.com with the subject "Sunny Harvest data deletion".
  2. Include your Player ID (shown in Settings) if you still have the game installed.
  3. We reply within 30 days and tell you what was deleted.

Also available: uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes the save on the device. To remove the cloud copy without the game, delete Sunny Harvest's data in your Google Play Games settings.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Game progress on the device (resources, upgrades, prestige, achievements, settings)Deleted by Reset Profile, uninstalling, or clearing app data.
Google Play Games cloud saveReplaced with a fresh farm by Reset Profile; fully removed when you delete the game's data in Google Play Games settings.
Purchases of permanent itemsKept by your Google account and Google Play, so they are restored on reinstall. Google keeps purchase records under its own policies.
Analytics and crash data (Firebase)Not linked to your name, email or Player ID, so we can't single it out. It is deleted automatically after at most 14 months.
Advertising data (AdMob)Held by Google. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and manage ad data in Google My Ad Center.
Support emailsDeleted on request; otherwise kept only as long as needed to answer you.
